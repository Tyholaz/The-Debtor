The call you hoped never came

You disappeared. Your old boss never forgot. A voice on an unknown number wants five hundred thousand dollars and gives you two days. You decide to talk in person, and you bring a pistol.

Shoot your way through five waves

A fast top-down shooter in pixel art. Never stand still, swap weapons mid-fight and clear the room before the boss walks in.

Pistol

Quick shots from range. Keep moving and pick enemies off before they close in.

Bat

Run faster and smash everything in front of you. Ten frames of pure impact.

Crowds

Enemies surround you, strafe and fire. Bullets fly from every side.

The boss

Five hundred health and a shotgun spread. He wants his money.

Move with WASD. Aim and shoot with the mouse. Press 1 for the pistol, 2 for the bat.

Kill him, or hear him out

At the end there is a choice. It changes the ending and the truth behind that call.
