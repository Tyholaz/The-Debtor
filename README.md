## The call you hoped never came

You disappeared. Your old boss never forgot. A voice on an unknown number wants
five hundred thousand dollars and gives you two days. You decide to talk in person,
and you bring a pistol.

**THE DEBTOR** is a fast top-down shooter in pixel art with a story told through
comic-style scenes. Forty-eight hours to settle up.

## Features

- **Five waves** of enemies that surround you, strafe and fire from every side
- **Two weapons** you swap mid-fight: a pistol for range, a bat for close quarters
- **A boss fight** with a shotgun spread and five hundred health
- **A choice at the end:** kill him, or hear him out. It changes the ending and
  the truth behind that call
